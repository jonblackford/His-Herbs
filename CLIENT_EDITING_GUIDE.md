# Client Editing Guide

The site is organized so the customer can update common text, service lists, seasonal cards, contact information, and gallery captions without touching HTML code.

## Best editing workflow

1. Open Pages CMS connected to the GitHub repository.
2. Open **Website Content**.
3. Update the relevant field:
   - Business Info
   - Homepage
   - Main Services
   - Florist Services
   - Greenhouse Items
   - Seasonal Cards
   - Gallery
   - Contact Page Intro
   - Contact Form Dropdown Options
4. Save/publish the change.
5. Netlify will automatically rebuild/redeploy the site.

## What to edit most often

- Phone number and hours note
- Seasonal cards
- Gallery photos and captions
- Services and descriptions
- Contact form dropdown options

## Inventory note

Avoid listing exact plant inventory unless the business can keep it updated. Use seasonal categories like annuals, herbs, hanging baskets, mums, and gift plants so the site stays accurate longer.
