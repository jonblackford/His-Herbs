About His & Herbs

Montrose Greenhouse — His & Herbs serves the Montrose area with a mix of floral design, greenhouse plants, herbs, seasonal color, plant gifts, and helpful hometown service.

Who they are

A full-service florist and greenhouse in one friendly local stop.

Warm, personal serviceSeasonal plants and flowersHelpful guidanceLocal Montrose rootsFlorist support year-round

The business brings together two things people love: flowers for meaningful moments and plants that make homes, porches, patios, gardens, and gifts feel alive. Whether someone needs a sympathy arrangement, a cheerful bouquet, spring planting help, fall mums, or a seasonal gift, the shop is designed to feel approachable and useful.

Selections naturally change throughout the year, which is part of what makes a greenhouse fun to visit. Customers can stop by to see what is fresh, call ahead for current availability, or use the contact form to start a floral or plant request.

Located at: 11440 Vienna Rd, Montrose, MI 48457

What makes the shop feel different.

  • Customers can get flowers for important moments and plants for everyday home beauty.
  • The greenhouse changes throughout the year, which keeps every visit fresh.
  • The shop can help customers choose gifts, arrangements, and plants that match the occasion.
  • The website is organized so customers can quickly find the information they need.

Planning a visit?

Open all four seasons. Seasonal hours and plant availability may change, so please call before visiting for specific items.
