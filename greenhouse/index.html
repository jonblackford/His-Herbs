Before you visit

Call ahead for specific plant needs.

Greenhouse inventory can change quickly because fresh plants move with the season. If you are looking for something specific, call ahead and the shop can let you know what is currently available.

  • Ask about current plant availability.
  • Bring photos of your porch, planter, or garden area for guidance.
  • Ask about light, watering, placement, and care needs.
  • Shop seasonally for the freshest options.
