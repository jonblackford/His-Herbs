Seasonal Favorites

A clearer guide to what customers can expect through the year, from spring planting and summer color to fall mums and winter floral gifts.

Four seasons, different reasons to stop in.

The greenhouse side changes naturally with the season, while the florist side helps year-round. Use this guide to plan when to stop in, call, or start a request.

Fresh starts for gardens and porches

Spring brings greenhouse color, herbs, vegetable starts, annuals, hanging baskets, and garden-ready plants. It is the perfect time to plan beds, pots, patios, and fresh curb appeal.

  • Annuals and garden color
  • Herbs and vegetable starts
  • Hanging baskets
  • Porch and patio plants

Bold color and easy outdoor beauty

Summer selections help keep patios, planters, gardens, and outdoor spaces feeling full and welcoming. Stop in for plants that match your sun, shade, and care needs.

  • Planters and outdoor color
  • Garden plants
  • Gift plants
  • Fresh floral arrangements

Warm autumn displays

Fall is a favorite time for mums, porch displays, harvest color, and seasonal arrangements. It is a great season for making entryways, events, and gifts feel cozy.

  • Mums and fall plants
  • Porch displays
  • Seasonal gifts
  • Autumn floral pieces

Flowers, gifts, and holiday warmth

Even when the greenhouse season slows down, the florist side stays helpful for holiday arrangements, centerpieces, sympathy flowers, gift plants, and winter celebrations.

  • Holiday flowers
  • Centerpieces
  • Gift plants
  • Sympathy and everyday arrangements

Why exact inventory changes.

Plants are seasonal and can sell through quickly, especially during busy planting and holiday weeks. These categories are a helpful guide, and calling is the best way to confirm what is fresh and available right now.

  • More accurate plant information.
  • Better seasonal guidance.
  • Less confusion about sold-out items.
  • Still helpful before you visit.
